Ground rules

  • Only texts customers who've agreed to hear from us. STOP ends it straight away.
  • Bank data is shared through open banking (CDR) with the customer's consent, read-only, and they can withdraw it at any time.
  • Options are general information. Jason confirms what suits Sarah before anything is applied for.
  • Questions the agent can't answer go straight to a person.
