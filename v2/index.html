Ground rules

  • Only texts customers who've agreed to hear from us. STOP ends it straight away.
  • Bank data comes through open banking (CDR) with consent, read-only, and can be withdrawn any time.
  • Options are general information. Jason confirms what suits Sarah.
  • Anything the agent can't answer goes to a person.
